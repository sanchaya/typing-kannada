ಸಂಕೀರ್ಣ ಅಕ್ಷರಗಳು — Complex Character Rendering

Kannada script does more than one letter per keystroke: consonant clusters, traditional ligatures, vocalic matras, nukta forms, and invisible joiners. Each card below shows the target text, what it's built from, the exact keystrokes that produce it, and its raw Unicode codepoints — useful both for learning and for checking how your font renders these.

ಪೂರ್ಣ ಯುನಿಕೋಡ್ ವ್ಯಾಪ್ತಿ — Full Unicode block coverage

Every assigned codepoint in the Kannada block (U+0C80–U+0CFF). Green-bordered tiles are on the selected layout's keys; the rest are historic letters and rare signs typed via a modifier (or composed by typing) — shown here as a font-rendering reference. If a tile shows a placeholder box, your font lacks that glyph.
